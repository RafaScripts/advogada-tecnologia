# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js, Tailwind CSS

## Users

Clients of a small tech law firm. The site requires a high level of accessibility, catering explicitly to visually impaired users (including the owner/advogada).

## Product Purpose

Landing page for a small tech law firm. To present services (LGPD, digital contracts, tech mediation, compliance), contact info, and about the professional, demonstrating knowledge of technology areas.

## Positioning

Elegant, modern, serious, showing deep knowledge in tech areas. Highly accessible.

## Operating Context

Visitors looking for specialized legal services in tech, LGPD, and compliance.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Must be highly accessible (screen readers, contrast, keyboard navigation). Uses Next.js and Tailwind CSS.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing texts on the landing page (Hero, Services, About, Contact).

## Product Principles

1. Accessibility is paramount (no compromises on contrast, semantic HTML, or screen reader support).
2. Elegant and modern design conveying seriousness and tech expertise.
3. Clear communication of services (LGPD, compliance, tech contracts).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Strict requirement: High level of accessibility. Must be fully usable by visually impaired users.
